This is an open-ended project:

Goal: This is a turn based rpg game where the user can create custom characters based on 3 different classtypes and use those created characters to either battle against each other or battle against computer players. Players can also display all the characters to the console, as they are stored and read off of a text file called characters.txt. As these characters win battles they earn experience which allow them to level up and increase their stats such as health, attack, and defense.

Classtypes: There are 3 different classes. Swordsman excels at being tanky, they start with increased health, and it's unique attribute is called Defend, which increases it's low starting defense by 3 each time it's used. The Wizard type starts with lower health but increased defense, it's unique attribute is that once it's health reaches 0 it is revived with 1 health allowing it to survive for one additional turn which can come in handy. The Archer has very similar stats to the wizard, but the archer doesn't get revived when it dies. It is unique in a similar way to the Swordsman, it has a Charge action that increases it's attack by a massive 4 points. Note: When the battle ends the program resets the stat increases back to original.

Battles: Battling is very simple. You can either choose to control 2 players against each other and battle them, or earn exp against a computer player using only one created character. During a battle there are 3 moves you can perform along with unique moves for each classtype as explained before. You can do a simple attack which deals a baseline damage of your attack minus the defender's defense. You can perform a special attack that deals almost double damage, but it has a 1/3 chance of missing. You can also use an item which simply recovers you 20% of your health, it can also take you over your health cap.
